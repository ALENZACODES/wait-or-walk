      How good is it?

      The model is a logistic regression trained on every subway delay the TTC logged from January 2025 to September 2026 (open data). It only uses what an alert tells you: the cause, the station, the line, the time and the day. Newer delays count more, and the forecast is recalibrated on the latest three months, because long delays became rarer in 2026.
