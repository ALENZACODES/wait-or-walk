      How good is it?

      What the number means. The TTC logs each incident with the minutes it delayed subway service. The number above is the estimated chance that an incident like this one, logged with more than 0 minutes of delay, delayed service 10 minutes or more. It is not your own remaining wait, which also depends on where you are and how service is restored. Records with 0 minutes of delay ( of them) are left out.

      How it was made. A logistic regression on every subway delay the TTC logged from January 2025 to September 2026 (open data), using only what an alert tells you: the cause, the station, the line, the time and the day. Newer delays count more. The base model is trained on everything up to , then its output is recalibrated on the next three months () and shipped as is, with no refit.

      Tested on months it never saw. Each test below runs that exact process: the base model stops three months before the test, the recalibration uses those three months, and the test months are touched only once, for scoring.
